SVD game sound adaptation
Source: SuperPhat, semiautorifleshot.wav, https://freesound.org/people/SuperPhat/sounds/421710/
License: CC0 https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
This is a game interpretation based on a designed layered semi-auto rifle sound,
not a recording of a real SVD. Based on previously approved Heavy/Metal treatments,
source starts at 0.185s. Lower-mid body, slight downward resampling, attack shaping,
gentle parallel saturation; 8 close variations per tone, not independent recordings.
No new reverb or noise layers. Full single tails retained. WAV PCM24 stereo 44100Hz.
Preview uses five manually spaced shots and chokes previous tail over 8ms at next shot;
the last shot has its full tail. Previews are outside the game asset folder.
Only shots included. SVD reload and bolt sounds have not yet been prepared.
